# Packs

A pack is a reusable **team**: a director (the lead who plans and synthesizes), specialist agents, knowledge, safety rules, and the **missions** the team knows how to deliver. Apps run packs; recipes show single agents. See [concepts](../docs/concepts.md).

| Pack | Missions | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`marketing-team/`](marketing-team/) | `autonomous-marketing-department` | Runs today through the kernel's built-in mission engine via [`apps/digital-marketing-team`](../apps/digital-marketing-team/) |

Mission templates loaded from packs are proposed in [libraos/libraos#1660](https://github.com/libraos/libraos/issues/1660); the restructure and an `investment-dd` pack are tracked in [#6](https://github.com/libraos/cookbook/issues/6).
