# Packs

A pack is a reusable **team**: a director (the lead who plans and synthesizes), specialist agents, knowledge, safety rules, and the **missions** the team knows how to deliver. Apps run packs; recipes show single agents. See [concepts](../docs/concepts.md).

| Pack | Missions | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`marketing-team/`](marketing-team/) | `autonomous-marketing-department` | Targets the kernel's built-in mission engine from [`apps/mission-console`](../apps/mission-console/) or [`apps/digital-marketing-team`](../apps/digital-marketing-team/) |

These examples do not load Runtime mission templates from packs. See [the cookbook pack proposal](https://github.com/libraos/cookbook/issues/6) for background, including an `investment-dd` pack idea.
